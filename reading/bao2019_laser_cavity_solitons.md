## Summary
Key concepts: 
- Describes experimental setup:
    - smaller ring cavity sat on a chip is nested within larger fibre cavity
    - pulse in the fibre cavity is spectrally limited via the erbium amp
    - smaller ring cavity filters light from the fibre, then broadens the spectrum via the Kerr effect
