**SUMMARY** 

## Frequency combs 
- The optical spectrum of a mode-locked laser is a frequency comb: discrete, equally spaced lines arising from the periodic pulse train the laser emits
- Line spacing = repetition rate f_r
- Phase and group velocities differ, so the carrier phase slips relative to the pulse envelope from pulse to pulse. This shifts the whole comb by the carrier-envelope offset frequency f_o
- comb equation: f_n = f_o + n f_r
- Both f_r and f_o are in the RF range, so measuring them links optical frequencies directly to electronically measurable signals

## Microresonator (Kerr) combs
- Combs can alse be geenrated from a CW wave pump by parametric frequency conversion in optical microresonators
- Microresonators confine light in small volumes, strongly enhancing intracavity intensity and nonlinear interactions 
- In whispering-gallery microresonators, light is confined by total internal reflection around the perimeter of an air-dielectric interface, reaching high Q-factor
- Threshold power for parametric oscillation scales approximately as 1/Q^2, so high Q allow comb generation at low pump powers

## Generation FWM
- Degenerate FWM: two pump photons are converted into signal and idler photons (remember SPDC) placed symetrically about the pump frequency 
- Cascaded FWM among these sidebands generates further equally spaced lines, building up the comb
